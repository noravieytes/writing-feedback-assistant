# Evaluation plan (draft)

Status: draft for the v0.1 pilot. The study will be pre-registered on OSF before any data are collected.

## Questions

1. **Accuracy.** Is the feedback faithful to the student text (evidence found verbatim, no invented errors)?
2. **Level fit.** Do ESOL teachers judge the next steps as appropriate "one level up" for the stated proficiency level?
3. **Usefulness.** Would teachers give the student-facing message with no edits, minor edits, or major edits?
4. **Time.** How long does it take a teacher to produce feedback with and without the tool?

## Phase 1: teacher review, no students (target: spring 2027)

- **Texts:** 30 synthetic or fully de-identified texts, balanced across levels 1-5 and the four Key Language Uses.
- **Raters:** 3 to 5 ESOL teachers, rating independently.
- **Measures per text:**
  - Evidence accuracy: share of quotes found verbatim (automatic) and share judged correctly interpreted (rater).
  - Level fit: 1-4 scale (too easy, about right, slightly high, far too high).
  - Edit burden: none, minor, major, unusable.
  - Harm check: any content that is inaccurate, discouraging, or culturally inappropriate (yes/no with note).
- **Agreement:** inter-rater agreement reported for level fit and edit burden.
- **Success criteria for moving to Phase 2:** at least 95% of evidence quotes verbatim, at least 80% of next steps rated "about right", at least 70% of messages needing no or minor edits, and no unresolved harm flags.

## Phase 2: classroom pilot (only with district approval)

Design to be defined with the district after Phase 1. Candidate design: within-teacher comparison of feedback cycles with and without the tool, measuring revision uptake (did the student apply the next step?) and teacher time.

## Reporting

Results, rating instruments, and de-identified aggregate data will be published in this repository and on OSF, including results that do not meet the criteria.
